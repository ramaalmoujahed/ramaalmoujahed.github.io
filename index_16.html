01 · Insurance Analytics

Insurance Risk & Claims Analysis

Problem
Identify which policyholder and vehicle factors drive car-insurance claim costs.
Approach
Single-page dashboard breaking claims down by vehicle use, car make, coverage zone, age group, kids driving, model year, education and marital status. A field-parameter dropdown switches every chart between claim amount and policy count, supported by DAX age binning, a conditional-formatting heat map and page-wide cross-filtering.
Result
Showed that private vehicles account for 80% of claim payouts.

02 · Supply Chain Analytics

Samsung Supply Chain & Procurement Analysis

Problem
Monitor the full Samsung product supply chain, from procurement to sales profitability, in one model.
Approach
Star schema with 4 fact tables and 6 dimensions. DAX measures for turnover rate, days of inventory, perfect order %, delivered %, YoY revenue growth and discount impact, using time intelligence and multi-condition CALCULATE logic.
Result
Six report pages covering procurement, production quality, inventory health, shipment reliability and sales profitability, with a measured perfect order rate of 75%.
